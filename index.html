Palm trees, thatch roofs and a pool in the middle of Rajkot.

Chef Fatso is an open-air cafe built like a holiday. Cottages with thatched roofs, clusters of cosy seating and lush greenery wrap around a small pool, and the lights come on as the evening does.

Everything on the menu is pure vegetarian, with Continental, Italian, Mexican and fusion plates made to share across a long table.
